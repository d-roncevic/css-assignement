Why CultureTrip

CultureTrip is a student-led cultural exploration project bringing together three unique countries — Kenya from East Africa, Croatia from Southern Europe,and Albania from the Western Balkans. Each page is researched and crafted by a team member from that country, giving you an authentic local perspective on travel destinations, traditional foods, and living cultures.

East Africa 📍 Nairobi

Kenya

A land of sweeping savannas, snow-capped mountains, and turquoise coastlines. Kenya is home to the world-famous Maasai Mara, vibrant Swahili culture, and some of the most flavourful cuisine on the continent. From the Great Migration to the buzzing streets of Nairobi, Kenya pulses with life and wonder.

  • ✓ Maasai Mara National Reserve
  • ✓ Nyama Choma & Pilau cuisine
  • ✓ Maasai & Kikuyu traditions

Southern Europe 📍 Zagreb

Croatia

Nestled between the Adriatic Sea and the Pannonian Plains, Croatia is a country of stunning contrasts. Medieval walled cities like Dubrovnik sit alongside cascading waterfalls at Plitvice Lakes. Croatian culture weaves together Mediterranean warmth, Slavic heritage and centuries of history.

  • ✓ Dubrovnik Old City & Plitvice Lakes
  • ✓ Black risotto & Peka cuisine
  • ✓ Klapa singing & Lace weaving

Western Balkans 📍 Tirana

Albania

Europe's best-kept secret. Albania's dramatic mountain ranges, ancient ruins, and pristine Riviera beaches remain largely undiscovered by mass tourism. Its people are famously warm, bound by the ancient code of Besa — a solemn promise of honour and hospitality that defines Albanian identity to this day.

  • ✓ Albanian Riviera & Butrint Ruins
  • ✓ Byrek & Fërgesa cuisine
  • ✓ Besa code & Iso-Polyphony music
